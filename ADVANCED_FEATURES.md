# Advanced Features List - Моят Глас

## Overview

This document outlines advanced features that can be added to the Моят Глас voting platform, similar to fundraising platforms. These features expand the platform's capabilities beyond basic voting into a comprehensive election management and engagement system.

---

## 💰 Fundraising & Financial Features

### 1. **Election Fundraising**
**Description**: Allow election organizers to raise funds for their campaigns or causes.

**Features**:
- Set fundraising goals (target amount)
- Accept donations via multiple payment methods (Stripe, PayPal, bank transfer)
- Real-time progress tracking with visual progress bars
- Donor recognition (optional public donor list)
- Automatic receipts and tax documentation
- Recurring donation support
- Donation tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold supporter levels)

**Use Cases**:
- Political campaigns raising funds
- Non-profit organizations funding initiatives
- Community projects requiring financial support
- Student council election campaigns

**Implementation Complexity**: High  
**Revenue Potential**: High (transaction fees, platform fees)

---

### 2. **Sponsorship System**
**Description**: Allow businesses/organizations to sponsor elections.

**Features**:
- Sponsor tiers (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze)
- Sponsor logo placement on election pages
- Sponsor profile pages with company information
- Sponsor analytics (impressions, clicks)
- Custom sponsor packages
- Sponsor-exclusive features (premium analytics, priority support)

**Use Cases**:
- Local businesses sponsoring community elections
- Corporate sponsors for student elections
- Media sponsors for high-profile elections

**Implementation Complexity**: Medium  
**Revenue Potential**: High (sponsorship fees)

---

### 3. **Campaign Budget Tracking**
**Description**: Track and manage campaign expenses and budgets.

**Features**:
- Budget allocation by category (advertising, events, materials)
- Expense tracking and categorization
- Budget vs. actual reporting
- Receipt upload and management
- Financial transparency reports
- Export financial reports (PDF, Excel)

**Use Cases**:
- Political campaigns with budget limits
- Transparent financial reporting for organizations
- Compliance with election finance regulations

**Implementation Complexity**: Medium  
**Revenue Potential**: Medium (premium feature)
